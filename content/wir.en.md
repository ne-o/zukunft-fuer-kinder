    text: |-
      Herbert and I raised six children. Each of them had their own interests, their own questions and their own pace. We were lucky to watch how their first years at school gradually turned into their own ideas for the future. Our children could learn, try out different things and find out what suited them. And when something didn't work out, they had the chance to try again or to choose a different path.

      Herbert and I were very grateful for that. Over time we became more and more aware of how much in a child's life depends on the circumstances they grow up in. Our children could go to school, were looked after when they were ill, and had people at their side who encouraged them. What was part of everyday life for us is out of reach for many families because of great obstacles. The question of how we could help other children get such opportunities had occupied us for a long time.

      For our silver wedding anniversary we travelled to South Africa. In Cape Town, a local guide took us to a neighbourhood where many families lived in difficult conditions. What we saw there moved us deeply. We had already thought about how we could help before the trip. After this visit, it was clear to us that we wanted to turn those thoughts into something concrete.

      A first step was sponsoring a child through Plan International Deutschland. As we learned more about the work of the children's rights organisation, we read on Plan's website about people who had set up their own foundation under the umbrella of the Stiftung Hilfe mit Plan. A foundation of our own gave us the chance to get involved for the long term and to support projects for children and young people in a targeted way. So in 2007, Herbert and I founded the Schönebeck Stiftung „Zukunft für Kinder“ under the umbrella of the Stiftung Hilfe mit Plan.

      Through our foundation, we support selected Plan International projects, mainly in Nepal. It is especially close to our hearts that children and young people can learn and get the support they need to develop. Today, our family carries on the foundation's work as volunteers. The wish behind it has stayed the same: we want more children to have the chance to discover their abilities and shape their own future.

      *Henriette Kolandt-Schönebeck, co-founder and Chair of the Board*

  - title: In memory of Herbert Schönebeck
    anchor: in-memory-of-herbert-schoenebeck
    text: |-
      On 14 March 2025, after a short illness, we had to let go of our beloved husband, dad, grandpa and father-in-law Herbert. We miss you. And we are grateful for everything we were able to learn from you.

      You showed us what humility means in everyday life: to notice the good in our own lives, not to take it for granted, and to stay attentive to how the people around us are doing. From you we learned to look, to listen, and to help whenever we can.

      You have left traces in our lives. What you lived by, we will carry on in our work for the foundation.

      For his funeral, Herbert had asked for donations instead of flowers. We were deeply touched by how many people followed his wish. The donations are intended for rebuilding a primary school in Sindhuli, Nepal, which was destroyed by a landslide.
