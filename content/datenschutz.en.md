_This is a translation. The [German privacy policy](/datenschutz.html) is legally binding._

We are pleased that you are visiting our website. Protecting your personal data is important to us. In this notice we inform you about how we collect and process your personal data.

## 1. Legal basis

The Schönebeck Stiftung „Zukunft für Kinder“ processes the personal data you provide on the basis of Art. 6 (1) sentence 1 letters a), b) and f) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of 27 April 2016 (EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR) and the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG).

## 2. Hosting

This website is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, GitHub processes technically necessary data (in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested and information about your browser) in order to deliver the pages and to keep the service secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably (Art. 6 (1) sentence 1 letter f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. You can find more information in GitHub's privacy statement.

## 3. No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use any analytics or tracking services. Fonts are loaded from our own website, not from third-party servers.

## 4. Contacting us by email

If you contact us by email, we store the data you provide (your email address and, where applicable, your name and telephone number) in order to answer your questions (Art. 6 (1) sentence 1 letter b) GDPR). You provide this information expressly on a voluntary basis and with your consent (Art. 6 (1) sentence 1 letter a) GDPR). Where this information concerns communication channels (for example your email address or telephone number), you also consent to us contacting you via this channel, if necessary, in order to answer your enquiry. You can of course withdraw this consent at any time with effect for the future. To pursue our legitimate interest (Art. 6 (1) sentence 1 letter f) GDPR), we also use your personal data to inform you by post about new projects.

## 5. Donations

If you send us a donation using the bank details given on our website, we store your name, your bank details and your address in order to record your donation and issue your donation receipt (Zuwendungsbestätigung). The Schönebeck Stiftung „Zukunft für Kinder“ issues the receipt itself and will usually send it to you by email or by post.

## 6. Disclosure of data

We do not pass your personal data on to third parties unless you have given us your express consent (Art. 6 (1) sentence 1 letter a) GDPR), we are legally obliged to do so in individual cases (Art. 6 (1) sentence 1 letter c) GDPR), or it is necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims and there is no reason to assume that you have an overriding legitimate interest in your data not being disclosed (Art. 6 (1) sentence 1 letter f) GDPR).

## 7. Storage period

Data subject to a ten-year retention obligation under tax and commercial law is stored for this period. We will delete your personal data without delay once the retention obligations and periods laid down by law have expired, provided that the purpose of the processing has been achieved.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to obtain information about the data stored about you at any time. If you have provided personal data, you can request that it be transferred in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.

## 9. Controller for data processing

You can request the correction or deletion of your data at any time, unless – in the case of deletion – statutory retention obligations and periods apply (see section 7). If you wish to object to the processing of your data, or if you have any other questions about data protection, please contact:

## 10. Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The supervisory authority responsible for the Schönebeck Stiftung „Zukunft für Kinder“ is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht), Promenade 27, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.
