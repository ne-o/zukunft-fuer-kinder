_**Enormous support last year**_

Many people in Germany complain about the rising cost of food, energy and much of what they need for everyday life. And indeed, here too we clearly feel the effects of the pandemic and the war in Ukraine, not least in our wallets. All the more remarkable is the unwavering support of our donors for our project work. Despite the difficult circumstances, we again experienced incredible generosity last year. Every contribution counts and enables us to provide concrete help. A very warm thank-you for that.

_**Loyal support in times of special challenges**_

In 2021, too, our foundation was generously supported by many donors. We are proud and grateful that we now have a stable base of supporters who, together with us, make sure that disadvantaged children get positive prospects for the future. We, the people responsible for the foundation, have the greatest respect for how such a culture is lived even in times of complete uncertainty for entrepreneurs.

_**Thinking of others despite the COVID-19 challenges in your own company**_

In recent months, companies have had to cope in particular with the restrictions of the pandemic. Often the effects show directly in turnover and profits, but even where that is not the case, hygiene concepts have to be followed and cooperation maintained at the greatest possible distance. Staff and managers are therefore challenged to drive business forward while looking out for each other. A role model in this respect is the RÖMERFAMILIE with its [promotional products business](http://www.roemerfamilie.de). As most company Christmas parties were cancelled this year, they had to handle a very busy Christmas season with their range – and they managed it with a good hygiene concept and a great deal of flexibility, despite the fact that many steps could only be done on site at the company, without infections or quarantine! Proof of responsible behaviour by all staff and the management. Further proof of the company's social responsibility is the support the RÖMERFAMILIE gave our foundation this year – despite stress and an above-average workload, those in need were not forgotten.
In Germany, one can certainly criticise distance learning and the constantly changing rules, but in the end there is state support for the vast majority here – from short-time work allowance and child benefit and other subsidies to COVID-19 aid for entrepreneurs. And so we must not forget what a lockdown means in developing countries such as our focus country Nepal: no school for the children and no income for the parents. Is there any doubt left about our obligation to help? Thank you to the RÖMERFAMILIE for being a role model for its staff and for other companies.

**_There can only be one sprint king – but many are happy about the donation (Jan 2015)_**

In December 2014, the athletics department of TSV Schwabmünchen again held its sprint king competition. What makes this competition special is that anyone can win: it's not about how fast you run, but about estimating your times as accurately as possible. Three different distances are run twice each (2 x 15 m, 2 x 20 m, 2 x 30 m), and the winner is whoever best estimates their total time. No interim results are announced. This year's best runner was off by just under 0.08 seconds – an incredible result. In the end, the sprint king's crown was handed to Michael Pfeifer by Wolfgang Ritschel, the department's running coach. And the foundation's coffers are delighted with the entry fees, which Ritschel handed over to the foundation's board.
