**_Our vision: children have a future!_**

Particularly the weakest in our society suffer most worldwide from poverty, sickness, war and missing social appreciation. A high illiteracy rate, child labour up to prostitution and little chances on a solid education are the consequences. Is that a life with future?

![](images/archiv/3128895.webp)

_We have the chance to help changing this._

If everybody provides a small contribution, something big can be created. Don't we have all so much that we can give a small amount? Wouldn't it be a different Christmas or birthday party if instead of a gift the feeling would make us happy that we've helped on a very concrete project?
The Schönebeck foundation strives to collect money for selected concrete projects. We have a strong professional partner – _[_Plan International Deutschland e.V._](http://www.plan-deutschland.de/)_

We make sure that our help arrives with least administrative expenses we it is needed and shows sustainably effect.

**One more child on our world with future - can there be a bigger thanks to you?**
