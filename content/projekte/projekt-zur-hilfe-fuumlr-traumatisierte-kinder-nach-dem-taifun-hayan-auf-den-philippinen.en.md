**_Project to help traumatised children after Typhoon Haiyan in the Philippines_**

Plan has set up more than 100 child-friendly spaces, where affected girls and boys can play, learn and come to terms with what they have experienced in safety, guided by trained staff. So far, around 20,000 children have received psychosocial support in this way. Plan's "cash for work" programmes involve almost 16,000 people in rebuilding their communities, securing their families' income.
Plan International Deutschland provides emergency aid in the Philippine provinces of Western and Eastern Samar, Cebu and Leyte.
