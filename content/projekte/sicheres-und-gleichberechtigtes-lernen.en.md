---
title: Safe and equal learning for girls and boys in Nepal
---

Although basic education in Nepal is compulsory up to grade 8 and even free up to grade 10, more than 660,000 children aged 5 to 12 do not go to school. Among secondary-school-age children, the figure is 1.6 million. Girls in particular are at risk of dropping out of school early. 37 percent of them marry before they turn 18 – a shocking 10 percent even before their 15th birthday.

The aim of our project is to give girls and boys access to high-quality education at safe and inclusive schools. We achieve this by creating a safe, child-friendly and gender-sensitive learning environment. Through targeted home visits, we also try to bring children back to school who have dropped out or who have never attended school because of special needs. So that all children – regardless of gender, caste or disability – can finish school, we carry out measures to promote gender equality and to end harmful traditional practices and attitudes. To achieve lasting, long-term success, we work closely with the local education authorities.

In total, 3,660 girls and boys aged three to 16 benefit from the activities.

Project region: Karnali province, districts of Jumla, Dolpa and Kalikot
Duration: three years
