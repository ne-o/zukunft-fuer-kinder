title: Careers and work for young people in Nepal

We launched this project to give young women and men a professional future, income opportunities and prospects in life. In it, we teach young people important skills for working life and the job market. 550 young women and men aged 18 to 24 take part in training courses in which they gain vocational qualifications and business knowledge. For participants with limited reading, writing or arithmetic skills, we offer literacy and maths courses. 275 young women and men also take part in English and computer courses. When selecting participants, we focus especially on disadvantaged groups – for example people who belong to a particular caste or ethnic minority and are affected by poverty. Young women and men who live in a household with children or adults with disabilities are also given priority. Because women in Nepal are often disadvantaged in their career and education opportunities and are paid less than men for the same work, we promote their equality on the job market. To this end, we train employers as well as government and public-sector staff in labour law, gender equality, and health and safety at work. We strengthen the young people's communication and leadership skills and their ability to cope with stress and conflict through so-called life-skills training. Courses on financial management and access to savings groups support their economic security and help them set up their own micro-businesses.

Project region: Dhanusha and Sindhuli in eastern Nepal
Duration: September 2022 – August 2025
