**What we want to achieve:**

The aim of the project is to improve health care for expectant mothers and young children in seven project communities in the Dolpa district. We also support early childhood education facilities so that children aged three to five are better prepared for primary school. Extending two health posts will provide around 250 expectant mothers and their children with care before, during and after birth. About 500 girls and boys up to the age of five benefit from the expansion of pre-schools and early childhood support.
Because of the severe earthquake in April 2015, building and safety regulations have raised the technical requirements, which has increased the costs. With the funds available, only one birth centre can therefore be built. Construction of this centre in the community of Suhun is 90% complete.
In addition, three pre-schools in the communities of Juphal, Liku and Pahada were completed and furnished during the reporting period. Construction of two pre-schools in the community of Suhun was also finished.
