---
title: Safe learning in Nepal
---

After the earthquake in Nepal in 2015, 47,497 of the country's 84,000 schools were destroyed or damaged. The Makwanpur region was hit particularly hard: 43% of its classrooms are no longer safe. With our project, we want to give children back a safe place to learn at school. We also want to strengthen the rights of girls and of children with disabilities, to create a protected and inclusive learning environment.

In total, 3,660 girls and boys aged three to 16 benefit from the activities.

Project region: Makwanpur
Duration: December 2017 – November 2020
