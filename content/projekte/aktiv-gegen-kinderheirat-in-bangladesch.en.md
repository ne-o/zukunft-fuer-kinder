Taking action against child marriage in Bangladesh

In no other Asian country is child marriage as widespread as in Bangladesh. For many young women, the consequences of early marriage are abuse or oppression and having their first child before the age of 19. These young mothers have little chance of a self-determined life with a job that can support them.
With the project "Taking action against child marriage", we want to help reduce the number of child marriages. Around 6,000 girls aged 12 to 18 are to build their self-confidence through targeted training so that they can resist being married early. They are supported in continuing their schooling and preparing for a profession. Parents, authorities, head teachers, teachers and religious leaders are also made aware of the serious consequences of child marriage. In total, this awareness-raising work will reach around 700 people.

**Results by the end of the project in 2017:**

- 2,300 schoolgirls strengthened their self-confidence and social skills in training sessions.
- 700 school classes and 25 teachers were trained to continue these sessions.
- 297 girls successfully completed vocational training.
- Around 6,500 pupils and adults were made aware of children's rights at events.
