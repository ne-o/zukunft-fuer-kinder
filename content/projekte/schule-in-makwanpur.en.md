**Building a school in Makwanpur**

From 18 to 28 October 2008, I took part in the founders' trip to Nepal together with one of our sons, Frederik.

Together with eight other foundation founders, Ms Kathrin Hartkopf, head of the Plan foundation centre in Hamburg, a photographer from Norway and a journalist from Hamburg, Frederik and I had the opportunity to see for ourselves the projects that Plan has set up or expanded in Nepal.

For us personally, the highlight of the trip was the inauguration of "our" school, the "Yug Chetana School" in Brindabau – Padampokhari in the Makwanpur district. Words cannot express what the children and teachers – and, not to forget, the staff of Plan Nepal – gave us there. We were welcomed with home-made garlands of flowers, hung around our necks "by the kilo", a tika on our foreheads and a constant, friendly "Namaste".

The inauguration programme lasted almost three hours. The children performed many songs whose lyrics they had written themselves, acted out sketches and presented numerous dances. Not many words were needed: the children's gratitude that they could now go to school was reflected in every face and gesture. It was hard for us to leave this place of joy and hospitality, but the packed programme urged us on.

Women's initiatives, bringing circus children back home, rainwater storage projects, Kamalari projects, visits to sponsored children, meetings with children's clubs and pupils, and women's education projects were just part of the rest of our programme.

Setting off home to Germany, knowing that we had to leave so much behind, was not easy for any of us. On the other hand, we took a lot of motivation with us to invest in new, urgently needed projects.

We are richer by a great experience and now appreciate our prosperity and standard of living more. It should be clear to everyone that nothing can be taken for granted – least of all the immense good fortune of having been born in this half of the world.

Plan also reported on our trip.
