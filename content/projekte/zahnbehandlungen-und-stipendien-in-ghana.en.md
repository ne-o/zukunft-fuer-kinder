**_Dental treatment and scholarships in Ghana_**

With the funds available, the Schönebeck-Stiftung „Zukunft für Kinder“ can provide concrete help for the following projects:

- More than two thirds of the students at Ghana's universities are male. We help young women to study at university as well.

- We enable gifted girls from disadvantaged families in Ghana to complete secondary school.

- We make it possible for primary school children to have a preventive dental check-up and, where necessary, follow-up treatment.
