---
title: Emergency aid during the drought in South Sudan
---

**More than a million children threatened by hunger**

For the first time in six years, the United Nations had to officially declare a famine – in Unity State in South Sudan. The food crisis in East Africa is devastating. Almost five million people, more than 40% of South Sudan's population, are suffering from hunger and urgently need food aid and support for their farming. The situation is so severe that many people's lives are in immediate danger. Children are hit hardest: one in three in the southern part of Unity is acutely malnourished.
Our partner, Plan International, has been working in South Sudan since 2006, in the states of Jonglei, Lakes, Eastern and Central Equatoria. The children's charity supports people with urgently needed food and provides life-saving humanitarian aid for children and families. Plan distributes food in four regions of South Sudan, supports farmers with seeds and tools, and plans to expand its nutrition programmes for children, pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers together with the World Food Programme (WFP) and UNICEF.
