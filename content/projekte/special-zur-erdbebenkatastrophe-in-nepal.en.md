---
title: Emergency aid after the severe earthquake in Nepal
---

**_Status report on our joint aid:_**

Together with you, other donors and our partner Plan International Deutschland e.V., we were able to provide targeted help. More than a million children were affected by the earthquake that shook Nepal in April 2015. Many people were made homeless and lost everything – among them many families and children in our project areas.

Our greatest concern was to get help to the people in the disaster area as quickly as possible. Our top priority was protecting the children, because girls and boys are particularly at risk in natural disasters.

Plan has been working in Nepal since 1977 and, together with the government and other aid organisations, was able to start emergency relief quickly.

Plan provided relief supplies mainly in nine affected districts:

-  Almost 6,000 tarpaulins for building emergency shelters
-  Almost 2,000 food parcels
-  In addition, blankets, mattresses, water purification solution, warm clothing and school materials
-  Five child-friendly spaces with psychosocial support were set up, benefiting more than 1,000 children and their families.

Together with Plan, we will continue to help on the ground and concentrate above all on the long-term reconstruction of Nepal. In areas where most schools were destroyed, we will support the building of temporary classrooms. Attending a temporary school doesn't just mean children can carry on learning quickly: it also gives them a safe place to stay and a little bit of normality back.
