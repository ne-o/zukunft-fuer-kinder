---
title: Sex education for young people in Malawi
---

Young women should be able to decide their own future. An important part of this is that, by planning their families themselves, they become independent of village communities that are often still patriarchal. They need to learn to make their own decisions and to exercise their rights in order to break with a tradition in which a third of young women and girls aged 15 to 19 have their first child, and 42% of these women marry before their 18th birthday.
We support Plan Deutschland in this large and wonderful project, in which more than 150,000 young people are to be informed, trained and advised. In addition, 50 savings groups for young people are being set up. The goal is to strengthen the sexual and reproductive health and rights of children, adolescents and young adults.

Project region: Mzuzu, Mzimba district
Duration: December 2018 – November 2021
