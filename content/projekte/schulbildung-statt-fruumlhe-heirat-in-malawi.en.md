**What we want to achieve:**

The aim of the project is to improve educational opportunities for girls in Malawi. Scholarships enable 20 girls from particularly disadvantaged families in the Mzuzu project area to attend and complete primary school. The scholarship holders receive educational support and live together in a girls' hostel.
Plan also supports the founding of girls' clubs, where schoolgirls can share their experiences and motivate each other. In addition, 76 teachers and 16 mentors take part in information and training sessions.
The project is part of the 18+ programme, with which Plan wants to prevent and, in the long term, end child marriage in Malawi, Zambia and Zimbabwe. The age at which girls marry is to be raised to at least 18. The programme "18+ Ending Child Marriage in Southern Africa" works at the legal and social level as well as at the individual level, with the girls themselves. Draft laws, changes in awareness, education about their rights and access to schooling are to strengthen girls and their position in society for the long term.

**On Valentine's Day 2017, a special milestone for children's rights was reached:**
**The loophole that allowed young people between 15 and 18 to marry with their parents' permission is a thing of the past. Marriage is now only allowed from the age of 18!**
