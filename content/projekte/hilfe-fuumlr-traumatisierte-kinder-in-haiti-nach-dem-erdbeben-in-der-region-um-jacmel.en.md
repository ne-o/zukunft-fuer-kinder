**_Help for traumatised children in Haiti after the earthquake in the Jacmel region_**

Concrete relief on the ground:

Our partner Plan is concentrating its emergency aid on the Jacmel and Croix-des-Bouquets regions, where – unlike in Port-au-Prince itself – only a few organisations are active. So far, tents, water and hygiene kits as well as other everyday items have been distributed to several thousand families in the two places. In various refugee camps, Plan also organises the preparation and distribution of meals with the help of volunteers from the communities. Psychosocial support for children through play and conversation has been organised in several camps, and 20 young volunteers have already been trained for this.

Prospects for reconstruction in Haiti:

Understandably, there are no detailed plans for the reconstruction phase yet. In line with Plan's expertise, however, they will include measures in all or some of the following areas:

- **Health care:** providing hygiene kits (soap, buckets, nappies etc.); setting up community-run health units that provide both health education and the detection and treatment of common diseases.

- **Water and sanitation:** creating safe water sources and hygiene education.

- **Child protection:** setting up medical, psychological, social and legal protection for vulnerable children and families, in close cooperation with local government bodies.

- **Education and early childhood development:** resuming school lessons in communities and camps in forms adapted to the special conditions.

- **Psychosocial support:** setting up children's centres in camps and communities that give children space to play and talk, helping them to come to terms with their trauma; developing violence prevention programmes; special services for people who have had amputations.

- **Participation:** actively involving those affected in planning and carrying out all measures, to avoid dependency and to strengthen people's self-confidence and sense of responsibility – and with it the sustainability of the measures.

- **Strengthening civil society and state institutions:** building the management capacity of government bodies with mandates relevant to children.

- **HIV/AIDS:** education and prevention; organising community-based care for people living with HIV and AIDS patients.

- **Income generation:** improving access to financial services, vocational training and markets; cash-for-work programmes.

- **Registration and replacing personal documents:** helping people get back birth certificates and other documents needed for health care and school; promoting birth registration in cooperation with the authorities.

Plan's special contribution in Haiti:

An important feature of how Plan works is networking and coordination with other actors. Working with different partners – whether local, national or international organisations – is a proven approach that Plan will take up again in Haiti as soon as possible. Thanks to its long-standing local presence and knowledge of local needs in particular, Plan can make a special contribution to humanitarian aid on the ground. Plan also has significant emergency experience (most recently in the hurricane season in autumn 2009), when it was the first organisation to provide help in Jacmel.
