---
title: Safe learning in remote mountain regions of Nepal
---

![](images/archiv/details-lernen-in-abgelegenen-bergregionen.webp)

Karnali province in western Nepal is one of the poorest regions in the country. The area is very mountainous and has little infrastructure. The long way to school is a problem, especially in winter, so an above-average number of children in Karnali do not go to school. Girls in particular are at risk of dropping out early, because they are often married at a young age. According to UNICEF, around 37 percent of girls and boys aged five to 17 are affected by child labour. Many girls also miss lessons during their period because schools often have no separate toilets for girls and boys. Children with disabilities face particular challenges, as most schools are not designed for their needs; a third of them do not go to school.
In the project "Safe and inclusive schools in Nepal", girls and boys from the districts of Jumla, Dolpa and Kalikot in Karnali province get equal access to high-quality education. Depending on what is needed, we repair school buildings and classrooms and build separate toilets for girls. We also set up accessible classrooms for children with disabilities, and we train teachers and parents on gender equality and inclusive education. In total, the project carries out measures at 80 pre-schools and primary schools in eight municipalities in the districts of Jumla, Dolpa and Kalikot.

Project region: Karnali province, districts of Jumla, Dolpa and Kalikot
Duration: three years (May 2019 – August 2022)
