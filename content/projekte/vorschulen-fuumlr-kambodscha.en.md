title: Pre-schools for Cambodia
---

![](images/archiv/kambodscha-vorschulen-f-r-kambodscha-pb-gno0516-image-5.webp)

The first years of life have a decisive influence on a child's physical and mental development. In rural Cambodia, however, parents of young children often lack access to early childhood care and education. Only 15 percent of children aged three to five attend a pre-school or kindergarten. Many children also suffer from malnutrition, which can seriously harm their physical and mental development. On top of this, the lack of clean water, toilets and hygiene facilities puts children's health at risk. With the project "Preventing malnutrition! Phase II", we provide early childhood care and education for more than 1,000 children in Stung Treng province. To do this, we train parents and carers in early childhood development, healthy nutrition and hygiene practices. To help improve the children's diet, we support families in planting vegetable gardens and keeping livestock.
At the health centres, we train the staff so that they can advise pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers on healthy nutrition and improve their skills in diagnosing and treating malnutrition. Working closely with the communities, we improve the water supply in the project villages and ensure that their residents have access to clean drinking water.

Project region: Stung Treng and Ratanakiri
Duration: November 2020 – October 2023
