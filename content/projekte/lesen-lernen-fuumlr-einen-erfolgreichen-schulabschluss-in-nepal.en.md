---
title: Learning to read for a successful school career in Nepal
---

**Project duration:** 24 months
**Project region:** the Dolpa district in northern Nepal
**Project goal:** thanks to a better learning environment, a total of 250 pre-school children and 750 pupils from grades 1 to 3 learn to read better.

To achieve this, Plan works with the local education authority and the families to improve the pre-school facilities and to equip the primary schools with learning materials. Primary and pre-school teachers are also trained in child-friendly teaching methods, and parents are more closely involved in their children's learning. Besides the 1,000 children, 120 teachers and 200 members of school committees and local non-governmental organisations benefit from the project activities.
