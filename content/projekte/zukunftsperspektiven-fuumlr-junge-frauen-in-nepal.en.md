---
title: Prospects for young women in Nepal
---

![](images/archiv/details-zukunftsperspektiven-f-r-junge-frauen.webp)

Every day, around 1,500 young people leave Nepal to work abroad. Poor working conditions, low wages and very limited job and training opportunities, especially in rural areas, drive them to leave their home. For young women, it is particularly hard to get a good education or job, which reduces their chances of earning their own income and leading a self-determined life. At the same time, Nepal has markets with great potential – among them tourism, with an annual growth rate of seven percent. The jobs in this sector also attract many young women and men from the surrounding regions to Pokhara, the project region. But not all of them find work. Companies often struggle to find qualified young people with the necessary skills.
With this project, we want to give 460 young women training geared to the job market. They include women who have come to Pokhara from rural areas, women from ethnic minorities and an expected 60 women with disabilities. All of them are to earn an adequate income as employees and benefit from better working conditions. In addition to the women, up to 300 employers take part in the project so that they provide safe and fair working conditions.

Project region: Pokhara in the Kaski district
Duration: three years (August 2019 – June 2022)
