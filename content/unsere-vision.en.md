**_Our vision: children have a future!_**

All over the world, it is the weakest members of our society who suffer most from poverty, illness, war and a lack of social recognition. The consequences are a high rate of illiteracy, child labour – even prostitution – and little chance of a decent education. Can that be a life with a future?

![](images/archiv/3128895.webp)

_We have the chance to help change this._

If everyone makes just a small contribution, something great can be achieved. Aren't we all doing well enough to easily give up a small part? Wouldn't Christmas or a birthday feel different for once if, instead of a present, we were made happy by the feeling of having helped with something very concrete?

The Schönebeck Stiftung „Zukunft für Kinder“ has made it its task to raise funds and pool them for selected, targeted projects.
_A strong, professional partner helps us with this – _[_Plan International Deutschland e.V._](http://www.plan-deutschland.de/)

_We make sure that the help arrives where it is needed with the least possible administrative effort, and that it has a lasting effect._

**One more child in the world with a future – could there be a greater thank-you?**
